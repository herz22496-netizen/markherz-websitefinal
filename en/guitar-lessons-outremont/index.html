The lessons in detail

Next door to a faculty of music

Outremont borders the Université de Montréal and its faculty of music. That piece of geography matters: here, a university audition is not a distant idea, it is a ten-minute trip and a jury that students from the neighbourhood face every year. The preparation is built over six to nine months, with a programme chosen according to the school's exact requirements.

A habit of private teaching

Outremont is a neighbourhood of houses and upper duplexes where musical training for children has long been part of the path, usually alongside school. Homes here generally have a room that can serve as a quiet working corner, which makes lessons at home particularly easy to arrange.

From the first lesson to the stage

The teaching covers the whole trajectory: position and tone for a child starting out, reading and theory, then, for those who want to go further, walking on stage, memorisation treated as a skill of its own, and stage nerves handled through repeated mock auditions rather than general advice.

What to have ready

  • ✓ A stable chair without armrests, and a music stand.
  • ✓ A quiet room, away from the traffic of the house.
  • ✓ A nylon-string classical guitar in the right size for the student.
  • ✓ A footstool or guitar support.

Frequently asked questions

Do you prepare students for Université de Montréal auditions?

Yes. Each school has its own repertoire requirements and theory tests, and the programme is chosen by reading those requirements together, six to nine months before the date.

Are lessons given in English?

Yes, in English, French or Czech, whichever suits the household.
